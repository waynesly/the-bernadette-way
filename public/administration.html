Platform settings and release readiness

Staff authentication: Individual Staff Accounts; administrator-only staff provisioning and password resets. Teacher access requires an active individual session and completed password change.

Student data: Existing student records are retained. This dashboard does not provide destructive database controls.

Deployment and site visibility: Managed through the Hatchable project console, not editable from this dashboard.

Release governance: Privacy and safeguarding review, leadership sign-off, and authenticated end-to-end acceptance testing remain outstanding.
