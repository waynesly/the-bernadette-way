Privacy and safeguarding

Information for students, families and staff. This is an application-use notice, not a replacement for the school's approved privacy, consent or child-safety policies.

Information used

Class rosters use student names and individual join codes. Learning pathways can save strengths responses, reflections, progress and reports. Staff Accounts use names, work email addresses and authentication records. Staff journals save individual professional reflections.

Safe use

  • Only enter information needed for the learning activity. Do not enter sensitive disclosures, medical information, student contact details or identifying information about other children in free-text reflections.
  • Keep join codes and printed student reports private. Do not post screenshots, reports, codes or identifiable student work to public websites or messaging groups.
  • Use your own Staff Account; do not share passwords. Sign out on shared devices and securely store or dispose of printed join cards.
  • Student codes allow access to student work. If a code is disclosed, contact the teacher or school administrator to arrange a controlled reset.
  • If a student raises a safety concern or makes a disclosure, follow the school's established child-protection reporting procedure. Do not use the reflection journal as an incident-reporting system.

Access and questions

Authorised teachers and administrators can access class data for educational purposes. Staff journal entries are designed to be private to the account owner. The school must confirm the authorised access roles, retention and deletion schedule, parent/guardian information and consent requirements, and incident-response contact before full rollout.

For access, corrections, privacy concerns or safeguarding questions, contact the school through its official communication channels. Do not send student passwords, join codes or private records through public forms.
