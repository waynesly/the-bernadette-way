A GIRL WHO SHOWED COURAGE

Meet St Bernadette

01

A humble beginning

Bernadette Soubirous was born in Lourdes, France, in 1844. Her family was poor. She often felt unwell and found school difficult.

02

A remarkable experience

When Bernadette was 14, she said she saw a beautiful lady at a grotto. Catholics believe this was Mary, the mother of Jesus, whom we honour as Our Lady of Lourdes.

03

Courage to tell the truth

Some people doubted Bernadette and asked her difficult questions. She stayed humble and honestly shared what she had experienced.

04

A life of faith and service

Bernadette later became a religious sister in Nevers. She lived a quiet life of prayer and service. The Catholic Church recognised her as a saint in 1933, and her example still inspires people today.

✦

What can we learn? You do not have to be the loudest, most popular or best at everything to make a difference. Your kindness, courage and honesty matter.

OUR CATHOLIC IDENTITY

Every person is precious to God

As a Catholic school, we believe each person is made in the image and likeness of God (Genesis 1:27). Everyone belongs, and everyone has gifts worth nurturing.

Jesus showed us how to love and serve. St Bernadette reminds us to be humble, courageous and faithful. Our strengths are not labels or rankings: they are gifts we can develop and share for the good of others.
